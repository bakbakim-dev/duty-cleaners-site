YOUR QUESTIONS ANSWERED
Frequently Asked Questions
Have questions? Here are some answers to people’s most common questions.
How long it will take to clean my house?
It depends on the type of home you have, its condition when we arrive, and the service you need. If the cleaning takes longer than the estimated time for your home, we will update you right away.
Are you insured and bonded?
Yes, we are insured and bonded.
Do you provide cleaning services for both homes and offices?
Absolutely! We cater to both residential and commercial spaces. Whether you need your home, apartment, townhouse, or office cleaned, our professional team will ensure your space is spotless.
Are your cleaning products safe?
Yes! At Duty Cleaners, we use safe cleaning products for your home and family. If you need eco-friendly products, let us know before your service so our cleaners can ensure they use them for your home.
What types of cleaning services do you offer?
We offer a wide range of cleaning services, including regular house cleaning, deep cleaning, move-in/move-out cleaning, office cleaning, and more. Whether you need a quick tidy-up or a thorough clean, we’ve got you covered!
