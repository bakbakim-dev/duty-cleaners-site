Frequently Asked Questions About Move Out Cleaning in Edmonton
Do I need to be home during the move out cleaning?
No, you don't need to be present. Just provide access to the home, and we'll lock up after we finish. Many clients prefer to schedule their move out cleaning after they've already relocated to their new home.
How long does move out cleaning take in Edmonton?
The duration depends on your home's size and condition. A 1-bedroom apartment typically takes 3-4 hours, while a 5-bedroom home may take 7-8 hours or more. We'll provide a time estimate when you book.
How much does move out cleaning cost in Edmonton?
Our move out cleaning services start at $189 for studio apartments and increase based on the property size and condition. Use our pricing calculator to get an instant estimate tailored to your specific needs.
Do you guarantee I'll get my security deposit back?
While we can't control all landlord decisions, our thorough cleaning meets or exceeds the standards required by most Edmonton property managers. If cleaning issues are cited for deposit deductions, we'll return to address them at no additional cost.
Should I clean before you arrive?
For move in and move out cleaning, the home must be completely empty before our team arrives — that includes all personal belongings, furniture, and trash. This ensures we can deliver a thorough, top-to-bottom clean without obstacles. If anything is left behind, we may not be able to complete the service as scheduled.
What's not included in your move out cleaning service?
Our standard move out cleaning doesn't include carpet deep cleaning, biohazard cleanup, pest removal, excessive debris removal, moving heavy furniture, or exterior window washing. These services can be arranged separately if needed.
How far in advance should I book move out cleaning?
We recommend booking 1-2 weeks in advance, especially during peak moving seasons (summer months and month-ends). However, we understand that moving plans can change quickly, and we offer last-minute and same-day appointments when available.
Do you bring your own cleaning supplies and equipment?
Yes, our professional move out cleaners bring all necessary cleaning supplies, equipment, and eco-friendly products on request. You don't need to provide anything except access to the property and functioning utilities (water and electricity).
