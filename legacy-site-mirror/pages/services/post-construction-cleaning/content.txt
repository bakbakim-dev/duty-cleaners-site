Dust-Free & Ready-to-Use Spaces After Construction
After construction or renovations, a space is left covered in dust, drywall residue, and construction mess. At Duty Cleaners Edmonton, we specialize in post-construction cleaning, ensuring your property is thoroughly cleaned, sanitized, and ready for use.
We provide detailed, heavy-duty cleaning for homes, offices, and commercial spaces, tackling fine dust, grime, and residue from surfaces, floors, and fixtures—all while ensuring a safe and professional cleanup process.
📅 Flexible Scheduling | Professional-Grade Cleaning | No Hidden Fees
What’s Included in Our Post-Construction Cleaning?
✔ Dust & Fine Particle Removal – Cleaning surfaces, floors, vent covers, and baseboards.✔ Deep Cleaning of Kitchens & Bathrooms – Wiping down countertops, bathroom toilets, tubs and cabinets.✔ Floor Cleaning – Sweeping, vacuuming, and mopping to remove construction residue.✔ Interior Glass & Mirror Cleaning – Removing dust and smudges.✔ Surface Wipe-Down – Walls, doors, window sills, and trim to remove leftover dust.
What We Do Not Offer
🚫 No disposal of leftover construction materials and debris🚫 No sticker removal from doors, windows, or other surfaces🚫 No cleaning of areas beyond the reach of a two-step stool
