Frequently Asked Questions About Move Out Cleaning
How long does move out cleaning take?
The duration depends on the size and condition of your property. Typically, apartment move out cleaning takes 4-6 hours, while houses may require 6-10 hours. We'll provide a time estimate when you book your service.
Will professional cleaning guarantee my deposit back?
While we can't guarantee your deposit return as it depends on your specific lease agreement and landlord requirements, our thorough move out cleaning service meets industry standards and satisfies most landlord expectations. We offer a satisfaction guarantee and will return for a re-clean if needed.
What's the difference between regular cleaning and move out cleaning?
Move out cleaning is much more comprehensive than regular maintenance cleaning. It includes cleaning inside appliances, cabinets, drawers, baseboards, and other areas not typically addressed in regular cleaning. It's designed to return the property to move-in ready condition.
Do I need to be present during the cleaning?
No, you don't need to be present. Many clients prefer to provide access and continue with their moving activities while we clean. We just need a way to access the property and clear instructions.
How far in advance should I book move out cleaning?
We recommend booking at least 1-2 weeks in advance, especially during peak moving seasons (summer months and month-ends). However, we understand that moving plans can change quickly, and we do our best to accommodate last-minute requests.
Do you provide cleaning supplies and equipment?
Yes, our team brings all necessary professional-grade cleaning supplies and equipment. You don't need to provide anything. Eco-friendly products are available upon request as an optional add-on.
What's your cancellation policy?
We understand that moving schedules can change. We require 24 hours' notice for cancellations or rescheduling to avoid a cancellation fee.
Do you offer same-day move out cleaning services?
When available, we can accommodate same-day service requests. Contact us as early as possible to check availability for urgent cleaning needs.
