What do you need to know?
What types of cleaning services does Duty Cleaners offer?
At Duty Cleaners, we offer a wide range of cleaning services tailored to your needs. Whether it’s routine residential cleaning, commercial services, or a detailed top-to-bottom clean, we’ve got you covered. We also specialize in move-in and move-out cleanings, ensuring every space is spotless. If you have specific requests, just let us know—we’re here to make your environment clean and comfortable.
Are your cleaning products safe for children and pets?
Safety is a top priority for us at Duty Cleaners. We use non-toxic and safe cleaning products, and if you prefer eco-friendly options, we’re happy to accommodate your request. Our goal is to provide a clean, healthy environment while ensuring peace of mind for you and your family. If you have any specific concerns about the products used, feel free to let us know.
How do I schedule a cleaning appointment?
Scheduling your cleaning service with Duty Cleaners is straightforward and hassle-free. You can easily book an appointment by calling our office, where our friendly staff will assist you in finding a convenient time. Alternatively, you can use our user-friendly online booking system, available on our website. Simply choose your desired service, select a time that fits your schedule, and provide us with any necessary details. We strive to be flexible and accommodate your needs, whether you’re looking for a one-time deep clean or regular maintenance. If you have any special requests or timing constraints, let us know so we can tailor our service to suit you best.
Do I need to be home during the cleaning?
You’re welcome to be present during the cleaning, but it’s not necessary. Many of our clients prefer to be out of the house while our team works, which allows us to clean without interruptions. However, if you prefer to stay home or have specific instructions, we’re happy to accommodate that. Just let us know your preference when scheduling. Our team is respectful of your space and will work efficiently to complete the job while ensuring minimal disruption to your routine. Your comfort and satisfaction are important to us, so we’ll do our best to meet your needs and preferences.
What should I do to prepare for a cleaning service?
To ensure a smooth and efficient cleaning experience, we recommend a few simple preparations. First, please remove any valuable or delicate items from the areas we will be cleaning. This helps prevent any accidental damage. Additionally, clear any personal belongings or clutter from the spaces to be cleaned to allow our team to work effectively. If you have specific instructions or areas that require special attention, let us know in advance. Providing us with clear access to the areas we’ll be working in will help us deliver the best results. Your cooperation makes it easier for us to provide a thorough and satisfactory cleaning service.
How do you ensure quality and satisfaction?
At Duty Cleaners, quality and customer satisfaction are at the heart of what we do. Our team is made up of experienced and well-trained professionals who are committed to delivering exceptional service. We maintain high standards by regularly evaluating our procedures and seeking feedback from our clients. If you’re not completely satisfied with our work, please let us know right away. We offer a satisfaction guarantee and will address any issues promptly. Your feedback is invaluable to us as it helps us continuously improve our services. We aim to ensure that every cleaning job meets your expectations and leaves you delighted with the results.
Can I trust my house cleaners?

Can I request specific cleaners for my service?
We understand that you may have preferences when it comes to who cleans your space. While we cannot guarantee that a specific cleaner will always be available, we do our best to accommodate requests for particular team members. If you have had a positive experience with a cleaner in the past or have specific requirements, let us know when scheduling your service. We will make every effort to assign the cleaner of your choice if possible. Our goal is to ensure that you receive consistent and high-quality service, and we value any feedback or preferences you share with us.
What is your cancellation policy?
We understand that plans can change, and you may need to cancel or reschedule your cleaning. To accommodate this, we ask for at least two days' notice. This allows us to adjust our schedule and offer the time slot to another client. If a cancellation is made less than 24 hours before the appointment, a $50 cancellation fee will apply. If you need to reschedule within this time frame, please reach out as soon as possible. We strive to be flexible and will do our best to find a new time that works for you while ensuring a smooth scheduling process.
How do you handle feedback or complaints?
Your feedback is crucial to us, and we take any complaints or concerns seriously. If you have any issues with our service or suggestions for improvement, please reach out to us directly. We are committed to resolving any problems quickly and effectively. Our team is here to listen and address your concerns to ensure that you are satisfied with the outcome. We value open communication and believe that addressing feedback helps us enhance our services and better meet your expectations. Don’t hesitate to contact us with any questions or concerns, as your satisfaction is our top priority.
