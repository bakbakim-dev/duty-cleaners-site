Things To Do In Chestermere, AB
Located just east of Calgary, Chestermere is a beautiful lakeside city known for its vibrant community and outdoor recreational opportunities. With a population of over 20,000, Chestermere combines small-town charm with the convenience of city amenities.
Start your day with a visit to Chestermere Lake, the heart of the community, perfect for boating, kayaking, paddleboarding, and even swimming during the summer months. For a scenic stroll or a bike ride, the Chestermere Pathway System offers picturesque views around the lake and throughout the city.
If you’re into golf, Lakeside Golf Club offers a beautiful course with stunning views and a relaxed atmosphere. Families can enjoy a fun day at John Peake Park, which features playgrounds, picnic spots, and a boat launch.
For shopping and dining, check out Chestermere Station, a bustling hub with local shops, boutiques, and cozy restaurants. Wrap up your day with a delicious meal at The Canadian Brewhouse, a local favorite offering hearty dishes and a friendly atmosphere. Is there anything you won't clean?
Our skilled professionals will go above and beyond to clean your home, but there are some tasks they won't perform for everyone's safety.
Here are some of the tasks we won't perform:
Cleaning light bulbs and lighting fixtures.
Cleaning chandeliers.
Cleaning outside windows.
Cleaning window screens or disassembling windows.
Doing laundry, organizing or folding clothes, and doing dishes.
Cleaning garages and patios.
Cleaning anything outside the home.
Moving or lifting anything over 25 lbs.
Stepping higher than two steps on a ladder.
Cleaning biohazards (mold, blood, bodily fluids).
Cleaning up animal waste.
Cleaning cat litter box.
Cleaning insects/rodents.
Heavy scrubbing of walls/doors.
Hoarding or cleaning high levels of trash/debris.
Furnace/vent/duct cleaning, upholstery cleaning, or drain cleaning.
Carpet steam commercial cleaning.
