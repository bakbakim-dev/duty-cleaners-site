Professional Cleaning for Newly Built & Renovated Spaces
Construction projects leave behind a mess—dust on every surface, smudges on windows, and residue on floors. At Duty Cleaners Calgary, we provide specialized post-construction cleaning that ensures your space is ready for immediate use.
We remove fine dust, clean surfaces, and sanitize key areas, so your property is move-in ready without lingering construction mess.
📅 Flexible Scheduling |Transparent Pricing
What’s Included in Our Post-Construction Cleaning?
✔ Fine Dust & Debris Removal – Wiping down walls, floors, counters, and vents.✔ Kitchen & Bathroom Cleaning – Sanitizing sinks, tubs, toilets and cabinets.✔ Floor Deep Cleaning – Sweeping, vacuuming, and mopping.✔ Interior Window & Glass Cleaning – Removing construction residue and smudges.✔ Surface Disinfection – Cleaning doors, trim, baseboards, and reachable-contact areas.
❌ What We Do Not Offer
🚫 No removal of construction debris or leftover materials🚫 No sticker removal from windows, doors, or surfaces🚫 No cleaning of areas requiring more than a two-step stool
