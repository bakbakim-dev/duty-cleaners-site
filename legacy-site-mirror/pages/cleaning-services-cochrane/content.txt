Things To Do In Cochrane, AB
Set against the breathtaking backdrop of the Rocky Mountains, Cochrane offers a unique mix of natural beauty and rich cultural heritage. Located just a short drive from Calgary, this town has grown rapidly while keeping its warm, small-town charm intact. With over 30,000 residents, Cochrane is known for its friendly community and scenic views.
Nature lovers can explore the expansive Glenbow Ranch Provincial Park, featuring miles of trails along the Bow River. History buffs will appreciate the Cochrane Ranche Historic Site, a tribute to Alberta’s ranching roots. For a sweet treat, be sure to stop by MacKay’s Ice Cream, a beloved local spot since 1948. Downtown Cochrane is perfect for a day of browsing charming boutiques and enjoying cozy cafes.

Is there anything you won't clean?
Our skilled professionals will go above and beyond to clean your home, but there are some tasks they won't perform for everyone's safety.
Here are some of the tasks we won't perform:
Cleaning light bulbs and lighting fixtures.
Cleaning chandeliers.
Cleaning outside windows.
Cleaning window screens or disassembling windows.
Doing laundry, organizing or folding clothes, and doing dishes.
Cleaning garages and patios.
Cleaning anything outside the home.
Moving or lifting anything over 25 lbs.
Stepping higher than two steps on a ladder.
Cleaning biohazards (mold, blood, bodily fluids).
Cleaning up animal waste.
Cleaning cat litter box.
Cleaning insects/rodents.
Heavy scrubbing of walls/doors.
Hoarding or cleaning high levels of trash/debris.
Furnace/vent/duct cleaning, upholstery cleaning, or drain cleaning.
Carpet steam commercial cleaning.
