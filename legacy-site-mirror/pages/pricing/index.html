At Duty Cleaners, we believe quality cleaning should be accessible and fairly priced. Our pricing is designed to provide flexibility and transparency, ensuring you get the best value for your cleaning needs.

We offer both hourly and flat-rate pricing options to suit your budget, with the flexibility of one-time or recurring services. Regular bookings come at a discounted rate, helping you maintain a pristine home effortlessly.

Once your booking is confirmed, we assign the most experienced team for your property. To ensure consistency and trust, we aim to send the same cleaning professionals for recurring services. Every service is backed by our 100% satisfaction guarantee—if you’re not happy, we’ll re-clean the areas at no cost. If concerns persist, we’ll make it right by addressing your feedback.

Factors That Affect Final Pricing

At Duty Cleaners, we recognize that even identical apartments have different residents with unique cleaning needs. While some services have fixed rates, many of our clients prefer customized cleaning solutions tailored to their specific requirements.

Clients can receive an accurate estimate before booking by providing a few details through our online portal or by calling our office. Rest assured, there are no hidden fees or surprise costs—Duty Cleaners prioritizes transparency and professionalism, ensuring a hassle-free experience at every step.

Choosing the Right Service(s) & Pricing for You

We strive to deliver 100% satisfaction by ensuring our clients get the most value from their booking. This involves assessing the unique cleaning needs of your home and family, and matching those needs with the best service options available.

If you need a quick refresh, a one-time service is ideal. For a thorough, deep clean to give your home a fresh start, our deep cleaning package is the perfect choice. For ongoing maintenance, routine or recurring services are perfect for busy homeowners who need consistent upkeep but lack the time to manage it.

Wondering which pricing option is right for you? Let us break it down clearly and simply to help you make an informed decision.

Hourly Cleaning Packages

Ideal for one-off cleaning needs or focusing on specific areas of your home, our hourly-based service is designed to address your priority list. This flexible package lets you customize the cleaning by selecting only the tasks you want done. At Duty Cleaners, we prioritize efficiency without compromising quality, ensuring meticulous attention to detail and outstanding results tailored to your needs.

Flat-Rate Pricing

This pricing model is a fixed rate for specific services, such as move in and move out cleaning, office cleaning, or deep cleaning. Pricing is determined by the number of bedrooms and bathrooms, as well as the overall size of the property. While the core cleaning tasks are standard and cannot be altered, clients have the option to add additional chargeable tasks to ensure the service fully meets their needs.

Why Choose Duty Cleaners for Your Home?

With over 8 years of experience providing office and home cleaning services in Edmonton and Calgary, Duty Cleaners has serviced thousands of homes! Backed by industry-leading satisfaction guarantees and a team of highly skilled cleaning professionals, you can rest assured that your property is in trusted hands.

Simply complete our quick online form with a few details, choose your service, and receive an instant quote before confirming your booking. From there, Duty Cleaners will take care of everything!

For any questions or assistance, feel free to contact us via phone or email!

Frequently Asked Questions

Have questions? Here are some answers to people’s most common questions.

Can I trust my house cleaners?

Yes, you can! We understand safety and trust are essential, especially when it's about having someone over at your property. That's why we have created a cleaning team that is reliable, caring, and carefully vetted!

What is Included in Maid Service in Edmonton and Calgary?

Our maid service in Edmonton and Calgary includes standard recurring cleaning to keep your home tidy, deep cleaning for a more thorough and detailed approach, and move-in/move-out cleaning to ensure your space is spotless for a transition. No matter which service you choose, our expert cleaners use high-quality products to deliver excellent results.

How long does a typical house cleaning take?

The time needed to clean a home varies based on factors like its size, condition, and the specific services requested. For instance, a three-bedroom home typically takes 3.5 to 4 hours with one cleaner, but this can extend if additional services are required.

Are there discounts if I provide my own cleaning supplies and equipment?

We provide all necessary equipment and supplies, so there’s no need for you to supply anything. Our discounts are only available during standard and deep cleaning services if you opt for recurring cleans:

Weekly: 20% off
Biweekly: 15% off
Monthly: 10% off
