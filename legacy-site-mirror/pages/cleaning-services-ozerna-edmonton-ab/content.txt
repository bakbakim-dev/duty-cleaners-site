Frequently Ask Questions
How much does cleaning cost?
Cleaning costs depending on the type and size of the home, the condition it's in, the type of service requested (such as standard, deep, or move-out cleaning), and any additional services added like interior window cleaning, baseboards, or appliances.
Do I need to be home during the cleaning appointment?
You don’t need to be home during the cleaning. You can provide access by sharing a code, leaving the key in the mailbox or under the mat, or giving us a garage code. If you’d like to do a walkthrough with our cleaners, we can call you about 30 minutes before they finish.
Is the Ozerna cleaning service insured and are cleaners background checked?
Yes, our cleaning company is fully insured, and all of our cleaners undergo a thorough screening process, which includes background checks and a series of assessments to ensure they meet our standards.
