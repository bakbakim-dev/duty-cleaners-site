Prepared 2026-09-20. This is a submission draft, not an instruction to edit or
contact an external account automatically.

## Why a correction is needed

The public ThreeBestRated Edmonton house-cleaning profile currently publishes
the old residential tiers (for example, $154 / $230 / $260 for a one-bedroom)
and says office cleaning is $60 per cleaner-hour. The current site and the
BookingKoala-derived price source do not support those claims. It also says the
company uses eco-friendly products without explaining that alternative products
are optional and carry a fee.

Source checked: <https://threebestrated.ca/house-cleaning-services-in-edmonton-ab>
