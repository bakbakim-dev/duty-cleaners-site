# Local-page overlap review — evidence gate

Date: 2026-09-19 (America/Edmonton)

## Decision

No local page is being deleted, redirected, canonicalized to another page, or bulk-expanded in this pass.

The available Google Search Console export contains separate query and page tables, not a joined query-by-page table. It therefore cannot show which two URLs receive impressions for the same query. The project also does not contain page-level booking, qualified-lead or revenue data. Similar wording or nearby geography alone is not enough evidence for consolidation.

## Pages that merit measurement, not automatic action

- Parkhill and Stanley Park: neighbouring Calgary pages with a plausible audience overlap.
- Black Diamond and Turner Valley: nearby town pages whose search language may overlap after municipal changes.
- A city service page and a neighbourhood page when both appear for the same service-plus-city query.
- Preserved legacy URLs and their modern route aliases: confirm the redirect/canonical behaviour, but do not treat an alias as a second content strategy.

These are review candidates, not findings of cannibalization.

## Evidence required before changing a page

Join, for at least the latest 16 weeks and the comparable prior-year period where available:

1. Search Console query × landing page × device × country data.
2. GA4 organic landing-page sessions and engaged sessions.
3. Quote starts, price reveals, qualified leads and completed bookings by landing page.
4. Booking value or another agreed business-value field.
5. The intended service area and whether the business actually accepts that booking.

## Decision rules

- **Keep both:** each page has distinct local queries or produces qualified bookings from its own service area.
- **Rewrite boundaries:** both pages are useful, but titles, headings or body copy target the same primary intent. Give each a distinct place and customer problem; do not add filler merely to make them longer.
- **Consolidate and redirect:** the pages satisfy the same intent, one has no distinct booking value, and the stronger page can serve both audiences without misleading local claims.
- **Noindex:** only for a page that users still need but searchers should not land on. This is not a substitute for resolving duplicate public content.

## Minimum evidence standard

Do not consolidate because two pages have similar templates, and do not expand because a page has few words. A change needs joined query/page evidence plus booking evidence. Record the before period, the selected primary page, every redirect, and the post-change result so the decision can be reversed if qualified demand falls.
